# GoreeCloud Documents — Privacy Policy

**Lifecycle:** Development  
**Scope:** GoreeCloud Documents source and planned product behavior  
**Production privacy acceptance:** Not established

GoreeCloud Documents is designed for highly sensitive personal, family, financial, legal, identity, administrative, and business documents. Privacy requirements therefore apply to original files, derived content, metadata, processing state, search indexes, AI-derived information, sharing state, and operational evidence.

## Principles

- Collect and process only document data needed for an explicit application purpose.
- Restrict access by default and keep authorization decisions bound to the underlying document and acting user.
- Preserve clear separation between source documents, user-authored metadata, and AI-generated or derived information.
- Do not let OCR, indexing, classification, embeddings, document Q&A, or other intelligence silently expand a document's disclosure boundary.
- Keep secrets, reusable credentials, and production environment values out of ordinary source history and document content.
- Make external sharing explicit, bounded, and revocable when sharing is implemented.
- Keep originals preservable independently from regenerated derivatives where practical.
- Define retention, deletion, export, backup, restore, and recovery behavior before production acceptance.

## Current Development boundary

The documented service is loopback-oriented and exposes bounded health/status diagnostics. Production authentication and authorization, user-facing sharing, AI processing, privacy-control surfaces, and accepted Privacy Shield runtime enforcement are not complete.

## Privacy Shield

Privacy Shield is the governing GoreeCloud privacy system for Documents. A Platform Contract declaration or source policy is not Privacy Shield runtime acceptance. Production privacy claims require exact evidence for the implemented data paths and accepted authorization/purpose/retention behavior.
