# GoreeCloud Documents — Competitive Objectives

## Original product boundary

GoreeCloud Documents must remain an original GoreeCloud-owned application at the product and architecture layer. Paperless-ngx and other document-management products may inform research, migration, compatibility, interoperability, and behavioral comparison, but they must not become the authoritative GoreeCloud product codebase or product identity.

## Objectives

GoreeCloud Documents should compete by combining:

- privacy-first self-hosting and explicit authorization;
- preserved originals and long-term archival integrity;
- OCR and rich full-text/metadata discovery;
- structured organization and document relationships;
- transparent AI-assisted classification and understanding;
- automation without surrendering document authority;
- granular collaboration and controlled sharing;
- portable export, recovery, and Everkeep-aligned preservation;
- native Glaze UI experiences; and
- first-party GoreeCloud platform integration.

Supporting libraries and engines may be used for formats, OCR, indexing, PDF processing, databases, cryptography, and standards compatibility when they remain behind GoreeCloud-owned contracts.

Competitive comparison never establishes feature completion or acceptance; exact implementation and validation evidence remains controlling.
