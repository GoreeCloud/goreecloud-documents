# GoreeCloud Documents — Benefits

GoreeCloud Documents is intended to provide the following product benefits as its planned capabilities mature and are independently accepted.

- **Private document ownership:** a self-hosted GoreeCloud application model with explicit authorization and privacy boundaries.
- **One document lifecycle:** capture, processing, organization, search, automation, collaboration, sharing, and preservation are designed as one coherent workflow.
- **Preservable originals:** original source files remain distinct from derived OCR text, previews, indexes, embeddings, and archival representations.
- **Powerful discovery:** full-text, metadata, relationship, relevance, and later semantic discovery are planned around document permissions rather than bypassing them.
- **Controlled intelligence:** AI-generated information must remain distinguishable from source documents and user-authored metadata and must not silently expand disclosure.
- **Portability and recovery:** Everkeep-aligned backup, restore, export, archival integrity, and long-term preservation are first-class requirements.
- **First-party integration:** Documents is designed to integrate with authorized GoreeCloud applications through explicit, least-privilege interfaces rather than third-party product ownership.

These are product objectives. This file does not by itself establish implementation, conformance, Stable qualification, or production readiness.
